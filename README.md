# 🎙️ Case Study: Text-to-Speech (TTS) AI Evaluation

> **Disclaimer:** *This repository outlines my experience and methodology as an AI Evaluator for Text-to-Speech models. Specific project details, model names, and proprietary audio datasets are omitted to comply with confidentiality agreements.*

## 📋 Project Overview
Served as an AI Evaluator and Quality Analyst for a Text-to-Speech (TTS) machine learning project. The primary objective was to assess and improve the output of synthetic voice models, ensuring high fidelity, naturalness, and linguistic accuracy for end-user applications.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities & Methodology
*   **Audio Quality Assurance:** Evaluated generated audio samples against strict quality guidelines, identifying AI artifacts, robotic tones, and audio glitches.
*   **Linguistic & Prosody Tuning:** Assessed pronunciation, intonation, and rhythm (prosody) to ensure the synthetic speech sounded natural and contextually appropriate in target languages.
*   **Data Annotation & Feedback Loop:** Provided structured, detailed feedback and ratings on model outputs to help ML engineers fine-tune the algorithms and reduce hallucination rates.
*   **Edge-Case Identification:** Tested the models with complex inputs, including homographs, technical jargon, and mixed-language sentences, to identify boundaries in the model's current capabilities.

## 🚀 Impact
*   Contributed to the iterative improvement of the TTS model, significantly enhancing the naturalness and accuracy of the synthetic voice outputs.
*   Ensured high-quality training data pipelines by maintaining strict evaluation standards and consistent annotation formatting.
